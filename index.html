01 · 认识古筝THE INSTRUMENT

上课前，先读懂这 21 根弦Before your first lesson, meet the 21 strings

Three things every beginner learns in the first lesson第一节课就会学到的三件事

五声音阶Five notes, four octaves

最常见的 D 调定弦只有五个音，一个八度里循环一次，21 根弦覆盖四个八度。第 1 弦音最高，离你最近。The usual D tuning uses only five notes, repeating every octave, so 21 strings cover four octaves. String 1 is the highest and sits closest to you.

1do2re3mi5sol6la

彩色的弦用来定位Coloured strings are landmarks

第 3、8、13、18 弦通常是彩色的（常见绿色），它们都是 sol。看到彩色弦，就知道自己在哪个八度。Strings 3, 8, 13 and 18 are usually coloured (often green), and each one is sol. Spot a coloured string and you know which octave you are in.

没有 4 和 7 怎么办Where are 4 and 7?

琴上没有 fa 和 si。左手在雁柱左边按下 3 弦，音就升高半音变成 4，这叫按音。很多流行歌里的 4 和 7 都是这样弹出来的。There is no fa or ti on the open strings. Press a 3 string to the left of its bridge and it rises a half step to 4. This is called an’yin (pressed note), and it is how many pop songs get their 4s and 7s.

指法Technique效果Effect

古筝“以韵补声”：右手弹出声音，左手让声音有表情。这也是第一节课就会让你感受到的地方。On the guzheng the right hand makes the sound and the left hand gives it expression. You will feel the difference in your first lesson.

04 · 常见问题FAQ

还在犹豫？先看看这些Still deciding? Start here

Questions students ask before their first lesson第一节课前常被问到的问题
我没有古筝，可以学吗？I don’t own a guzheng. Can I still learn?

可以。体验课和前几节课都用老师的琴。等你确定想继续学，我可以帮你挑选租琴或二手琴的渠道，避免一开始就花大钱。Yes. The trial and your first few lessons use my instrument. Once you know you want to continue, I can point you to rental or second-hand options so you don’t spend a lot up front.

在哪里上课？可以线上吗？Where are lessons? Can I learn online?

线下课在 UBC 校园附近，预约后告诉你具体地点。家里已经有琴的同学也可以上线上课。In-person lessons are near the UBC campus; you get the exact location after booking. If you already have an instrument, online lessons are available too.

需要会看简谱或五线谱吗？Do I need to read music?

不需要。古筝谱大多是简谱，就是上面那行 3 3 5 6 的数字，第一节课就能学会看。No. Most guzheng music is written in jianpu, the numbered notation you saw above (3 3 5 6). You will read it by the end of your first lesson.

学什么曲子？What will I play?

以流行歌曲为主。你告诉我想弹哪首，我按你的程度编成古筝版，一直学到能完整弹下来的成品。Mainly pop songs. Tell me the song you want to play and I’ll arrange it for your level on the guzheng, then we work on it until you can play the whole thing.

上课用什么语言？What language are lessons in?

普通话或英语都可以。English or Mandarin, whichever you prefer.

多久能弹一首完整的曲子？How soon can I play a full piece?

零基础每周练习 3–4 次，通常 1–2 个月就能弹几首简单的流行歌曲。学过的同学通常会快很多。Complete beginners who practise 3–4 times a week can usually play a few simple pop songs within 1–2 months. Returning players are usually much faster.
